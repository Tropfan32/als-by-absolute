Tuning guide

Tune Pedro first (localization, then translational / heading / drive PID). AACS cannot save a follower that cannot hold a line.

ImpactDetector​

SymptomChange
False triggers on launch / strafeRaise setThresholdG (try 1.8–2.2) or setSustainWindowMs (70–100)
Real hits ignoredLower G threshold or shorten the window (not below ~30 ms at 50 Hz)
Hits during planned brakingUse update(ax, ay, expectedAx, expectedAy) from path accel
ImpactDetector detector = new ImpactDetector(1.8); // g
detector.setSustainWindowMs(70);

Log getLastHorizontalAccelG() on the DS while you drive. Set the threshold above peak vibration and below a real T-bone.

AdaptivePathPlanner​

SymptomChange
Path rebuilds every cycle while touchingIncrease setCooldownMs (400–500)
Recovery jerks sidewaysLower setControlPointFraction (0.2–0.3) or check velocity sign
Replan skipped next to the goalsetMinReplanDistanceInches
Need to hold the end posesetHoldEnd(true) (default)

FoulPreventionBox​

SymptomChange
Recovery hugs walls / clips tilesIncrease setClearanceInches (robot half-width + margin)
Legal shortcuts rejectedShrink AABBs; they should match foul regions, not scoring areas
Start and goal separated by a fat boxAACS will skip; split Auto into two legal paths

:::caution Match units Field and zones are Pedro inches. Pinpoint offsets in Constants are millimeters. Accel into the detector is m/s². Mixing these is the usual “it teleports / never triggers” bug. :::
